# Local listing kit: Patel Selection

**Honest note:** a listing can only be created by you. Google, Bing, Apple and JustDial each verify the owner by phone, postcard or video, so nobody else (including me) can submit them for you. This file has everything ready to paste, so each listing takes about 10 minutes.

## 1. The details to use everywhere (copy exactly)

Use the *same* name, address and phone on every site. Small differences hurt local ranking.

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Business name | Patel Selection |
| Address | Opp. Tata Motors Show Room, Nr. Mercedes Show Room, Ambli-Bopal Road, Ahmedabad, Gujarat 380058 |
| Phone | +91 97125 92905 |
| Owner | Arvind Patel |
| Website | your real domain (see step 0) |

**Do not** add keywords to the business name (for example "Patel Selection Best Plastic Chairs"). Google suspends profiles for that.

## 0. Before you start

1. Buy a real domain (for example `patelselection.in`). The site is currently set to `patel-selection.pages.dev`. Put the new address in `site.config.json`, rebuild and redeploy.
2. Decide your shop timings. Wrong timings cost you customers and ranking, so do not guess.
3. Take photos (see section 3).

## 2. Google Business Profile (most important, free)

1. Go to business.google.com, search for "Patel Selection". If it already exists, **claim** it. If not, **add** it.
2. Pick the shop-location option (customers visit you). Enter the address above and drop the pin on the exact shop entrance.
3. Verify (phone, SMS, video or postcard, whichever Google offers).

**Categories** (names change from time to time, so pick the closest ones in the dropdown):
- Primary: Housewares store (or Furniture store, whichever you earn more from)
- Additional: Furniture store, Kitchen supply store, Plastic products supplier, Home goods store

- Get your review link from the profile ("Ask for reviews") and make a QR code for the counter.
- Send it after every sale on WhatsApp. Do **not** pay for or write fake reviews; Google removes them and can suspend the profile.

English: `Thank you for shopping at Patel Selection! If you are happy with your purchase, please leave us a Google review here: <link>`

## 8. Tell Google about the website

1. Open search.google.com/search-console and add your domain.
2. Submit `https://YOURDOMAIN/sitemap.xml` (the build creates it, with all 192 page versions in English, Hindi and Gujarati).
3. In "URL inspection", paste the home page and 5 key pages (`/products/chairs`, `/products/dining-tables`, `/visit` and so on) and click "Request indexing".
4. Do the same in Bing Webmaster Tools (you can import from Search Console).
5. Check back after 1 to 2 weeks under "Pages" for any problems.

## 9. What to expect

Nobody can promise the number 1 position. For searches like "plastic chair shop near me", the map results depend on how relevant your profile is, how close you are, and how well known you are (reviews, mentions, links). The site is now built so Google can read every product, in three languages. The profile, reviews and photos are the part only you can add, and they matter just as much.
